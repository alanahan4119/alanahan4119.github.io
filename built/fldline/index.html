Case study 03 · 0-to-1 product development

FLDLine

One place for small trade crews to manage work from the first customer call to a draft invoice.

Founder & product lead · Field service software · Live demo launched November 18, 2026

The problem

For independent contractors and small trade crews, the details of a job can be spread across text messages, spreadsheets, and paper notes. That makes it harder for the office and the crew to see who is assigned, what work is complete, and what still needs to be billed.

Why I founded FLDLine

I came up with the idea for FLDLine and founded it to solve that problem. I set the product direction, defined the first release, and decided how scheduling, time tracking, and invoicing should work together. I used AI to help build the product and led decisions on usability, acceptance criteria, and quality.

What shipped

The demo lets visitors work through a job using sample data. They can add a customer, schedule a job, assign a crew, log hours, and create a draft invoice from the job record. The schedule flags overlapping assignments, and jobs support fixed-price or time-and-materials billing.

A product decision that mattered

I wanted invoices to reflect the rate in effect when work was logged. FLDLine saves the billable rate with each time entry, so a later rate change leaves earlier entries intact. The app also flags hours logged after invoicing so the team can review the difference.

Launch and availability

The FLDLine demo went live on November 18, 2026. Visitors can try the workflow from scheduling through draft invoicing in a resettable workspace with sample jobs.

The production platform is planned for early 2027, when contractors will be able to sign up directly to manage their own jobs, crews, and invoices.
