About me

I build products, solve business problems, and help teams become better at what they do.

Throughout my career, I've worked across product strategy, technology, and business operations. I've led complex programs, brought new products to market, and worked with teams to improve how we plan, develop, and deliver software.

I enjoy getting into the details. Whether it's working through a technical challenge, evaluating a new product opportunity, or finding ways to improve how a business operates, I like understanding the problem and being directly involved in finding a solution.

At the same time, I believe one of my most important responsibilities as a leader is developing the people around me. I want my teams to feel comfortable making decisions, challenging ideas, and taking ownership of their work. My job is to provide direction, remove obstacles, and make sure they have the tools and support to be successful.

I've had the opportunity to improve delivery timelines, reduce operating costs, strengthen product quality, and help bring new products to market. I'm proud of those accomplishments, but I'm equally proud of the teams I've helped develop and the people who've grown into greater responsibilities along the way.

For me, success isn't just about what we deliver. It's also about building a team that's more capable, confident, and successful because of the work we've done together.

How I work

Lead with trust. Build ownership. Grow teams.

I believe a leader’s job is to create an environment where people have the clarity, trust, and support to do their best work.

That means setting a clear direction, removing obstacles, and giving people the opportunity to take ownership rather than simply follow instructions.

I hold myself accountable for the team’s outcomes while giving individuals room to make decisions, challenge assumptions, and grow their skills. I want people to understand not just what we’re building, but why it matters and how their work contributes to the bigger picture.

I’ve found that strong teams aren’t built by adding more oversight. They’re built by developing capable people, sharing knowledge, establishing clear expectations, and creating opportunities for others to lead.

My goal is to build teams that become more confident, capable, and independent over time.
