Case study 02 · SaaS product strategy

Consent On Demand

Turning an industry challenge into a commercial SaaS product.

Big Wave Systems · Product Owner / Product Strategy & Business Operations · Healthcare technology

500+Registered users
~30%Reported paid-subscription share
LimitedPaid marketing investment

The opportunity

The idea emerged at a CMS Agent/Broker Summit, where concerns about fraud and the possible need for consent for individual agent and broker actions highlighted a practical problem. We saw an opportunity to make requesting, documenting, and managing consumer consent simpler for both agents and the people they serve.

My role

I worked with business and technical teams to translate the opportunity into product and technical requirements, oversee the build, establish ownership, and keep teams aligned on product direction. I partnered with Sales and Marketing on rollout planning and positioning.

The product

Consent On Demand allows agents and brokers to send consent requests by SMS or email. Consumers can review and electronically sign or reject a request. The platform supports reusable templates, additional team users, countersignatures, and visibility into each request's status and outstanding actions.

Pricing and commercialization

I worked with Sales leadership to evaluate subscription tiers, pricing, and packaging, balancing accessibility and scalability with the recurring costs of operating, maintaining, and improving the platform. We continued to adjust the model as product and business needs evolved.

Early traction

The platform has grown to more than 500 registered users, with approximately 30% reported as distinct paid subscriptions. This early adoption has come with minimal marketing spend, primarily small Facebook and LinkedIn campaigns. Registered users and paid subscriptions are different measures; the percentage is presented as reported rather than converted into an inferred subscription count.

Exploring AI-enabled workflows

I'm personally leading an exploration with our development leader into AI capabilities that could help agents and brokers draft consent messages, understand request status, identify consents requiring follow-up, and generate reminders. These capabilities are being evaluated and have not been released.

What it demonstrates

This work brings together early product discovery, cross-functional development leadership, pricing decisions, commercialization, and ongoing product strategy—while balancing customer needs with the economics of running a SaaS platform.
